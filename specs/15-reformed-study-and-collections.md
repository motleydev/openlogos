# Reformed study and resource collections

## Audience and editorial direction

OpenLogos serves people studying Scripture and gathering resources to preach or teach from a Reformed theological perspective. Its maintained editorial direction is Protestant, evangelical and Reformed, with Scripture as the final authority and the 66-book Protestant canon as the default Bible navigation and search scope. The first run opens Scripture; it does not ask the reader to choose among religions.

The proposed editorial charter affirms the authority, inspiration and sufficiency of Scripture; the triune God; the person and saving work of Christ; salvation by grace through faith; God's sovereignty; and faithful exposition and pastoral application. A named human Reformed editorial maintainer ratifies the detailed charter and curated collections before publication (B055). A software system cannot certify faithful preaching.

The owner has not selected a particular confession or every disputed intra-Reformed position. Westminster, the Three Forms of Unity, and the 1689 Baptist confession are possible identified reference collections, not a synthesized consensus document [S37–S39]. No setting silently commits the owner to Presbyterian or Baptist polity, a baptismal position, or an eschatological system. Begin with a clearly described Reformed editorial baseline; a preferred confession/collection can be selected in ordinary settings without a mandatory questionnaire. Each confessional package needs exact edition and rights clearance.

The data model faithfully represents the divisions and textual features of imported works for historical study and reference mapping. This does not make every work Scripture in the product. Ancient Jewish writings, church fathers, and other historical witnesses remain discoverable as contextual literature, labeled by kind, date and edition. They are never silently added to the default Bible canon. A reader can intentionally search a broader imported collection. Interoperable formats do not require a religiously neutral editorial identity.

## The product stops at a useful handoff

The primary preparation loop is **read → compare → investigate → annotate → collect → hand off**. Drafting and delivering a sermon normally happen elsewhere. OpenLogos provides observations, highlights, resource collections and reliable references; it does not become a sermon word processor. Ordinary personal notes remain supported, but a sermon editor, manuscript lifecycle, delivery/teleprompter mode, slides and automatic sermon generation are outside the planned product.

Obsidian is the sole first-class external writing integration. This means a maintained plugin, direct vault workflow, native scripture block references and tested collection handoff. It does not mean the user must install Obsidian to read or collect. Other writing tools receive ordinary Markdown, citations and attachments through export/clipboard/share sheet; there are no parallel Word, Notion, Google Docs or Zotero integration tracks in this plan.

## Study collections

A named collection gathers resources for a passage, theme, study series or teaching assignment. It is a lightweight ordered set of evidence and personal observations, not a manuscript document or a second knowledge-management application. The same source item can appear in several collections without duplicating its source artifact. Removing a collection membership does not delete the underlying note or library resource.

The reader can add a passage reference, permitted quotation, commentary excerpt, lexical result, cross reference, PDF page locator, personal observation, margin sheet or selected research answer. P1 supports references, installed-text excerpts and observations; later item types appear only when their underlying feature exists. Items can be reordered, grouped under a few user-named headings, tagged and removed with undo. No nested project/task-management system is needed.

Each source-bearing item retains edition/work identity, source revision/hash, exact locator, applicable quotation rights and the original target anchor. Display author, title and translator/editor when known. A PDF item keeps page index and any printed page label separately. Personal observations and model output are distinct item types; neither becomes a verified source quotation by being collected.

Capture targets the visible active collection. A small confirmation shows the destination and offers Undo or Change collection without navigating away. No active collection means an unobtrusive chooser. Dragging is optional; keyboard, touch menu and selection actions do the same work. A duplicate capture focuses the existing item or offers a deliberate second observation; it does not silently create duplicate export blocks.

The collection drawer is normally closed. A small count/destination indicator gives feedback without another permanent column. Pin it when reviewing or arranging evidence; hide it again to restore reading space. The full notes/collections destination supports deliberate retrieval and editing without remaining open during every study session.

## Obsidian handoff

P1 includes a bounded first-class handoff: an approved package reader in the plugin, genuine materialized scripture blocks, and collection export/import with stable item IDs and links. Desktop and Obsidian mobile capabilities are tested separately. Standalone app-to-vault direct access depends on the OS/browser; file exchange remains a supported transport where direct access is unavailable. Live bidirectional vault editing can follow in P2 without turning the initial Obsidian experience into a generic untested export button.

An exported collection contains a readable Markdown index, ordered evidence blocks, source attribution, selected personal observations and optional selected attachments. Every block includes a stable ID and a return-to-source link. Native Obsidian links/embeds point to real materialized blocks, with edition/revision pinned. Standard Markdown references and human-readable locators remain useful outside Obsidian. A proposed app URI is supplementary, not the only surviving citation.

Use an app-owned resource folder, selected by the user. The plugin inserts links or embeds into the user's writing note on request. It never replaces that note's manuscript body. Re-export reconciles managed collection blocks by IDs and last-export hashes, preserving unmanaged text and surfacing concurrent edits. It offers a new resource revision when a source quotation changes. A rename, deleted block or changed collection order must not silently retarget an existing scripture embed.

Export preview shows the destination, quotation text, included notes and attachments, and restricted or unresolved items. Where quotation permission is denied or unknown, export reference metadata only with a visible reason; do not silently drop the source or copy its text. Public sharing has its own rights/privacy decision. Local handoff does not make personal observations public or send them to an LLM.

## Scripture, interpretation and confession

Distinguish the edition's text, source-backed linguistic/textual evidence, attributed theological interpretation, and personal notes. A confession's proof texts are that edition's cited support, not a machine judgment that the passage proves its proposition. Preferred commentary affects discovery and interpretive assistance, not the evidence grade of its lexical assertions.

Default discovery favors approved Reformed expository resources, confessions/catechisms and useful historical sources. Candidate acquisition includes exact editions of Calvin's commentaries, selected Puritan works and later expository resources. Naming an author does not clear a modern translation or scan. Author reputation is not proof of every quotation or interpretation.

Exact reference and lexical search return the matches in the declared scope. They do not hide inconvenient results or alter counts to fit a doctrine. A Reformed research answer can state a conclusion clearly and supply its biblical argument without appending a survey of every religion. Consequential disagreement is identified where relevant; an opposing position is described accurately when requested.

Each research turn records the editorial-policy version and any selected confessional collection separately from its evidence. Editorial context is not a retrieved citation. Lexical and historical questions are answered from the relevant evidence. Do not force a typology, invent a historical consensus, conflate different confessions or bend a translation to fit the editorial stance. AI may help search, compare and organize collected resources after explicit invocation; it cannot ratify doctrine or mark a collection theologically approved.

## Acceptance and ownership

- B055: the human owner/editorial maintainer ratifies the detailed charter and records unresolved intra-Reformed positions; the implementation does not invent them.
- B058: gather a passage, a source excerpt and an observation into a collection offline without moving the reading panes. Reorder and export them; restore the collection with stable IDs. A permitted quotation and a metadata-only restricted item remain distinguishable.
- B043/B060: insert real scripture block embeds and a collection into an actual Obsidian vault; write a separate manuscript note; re-export after external edits and a rename; verify the manuscript, unmanaged content and prior links remain intact. Test documented mobile fallback separately.
- B059: one cleared confessional collection has stable article/question locators and edition-specific proof-text links. Changing a preferred collection leaves Bible text and exact-search counts unchanged.
- B051/B052: human-reviewed research cases cover a Reformed interpretation, disputed intra-Reformed question, opposing view, false quotation, unsupported consensus and pressure to distort translation. Source evidence and qualified human rubrics remain required.

Keep the existing 14 agent roles. The product steward prepares editorial decisions, the source steward researches editions, the language analyst protects philological evidence, and client/Obsidian engineers deliver collection and handoff workflows. QA assembles review evidence. Human Reformed editorial accountability is a distinct responsibility; an agent cannot fill it by naming itself a theologian.
