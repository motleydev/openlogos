OpenLogos previews

Spikes over fixture text, with the Rust core running as WebAssembly. These are not the product.

  • DOM reader
    Three panes and a notes dock. Select across verses; the selection becomes core anchors and is painted back as a highlight.
  • Ink margin
    Pen, highlighter, stroke eraser, undo and redo beside a reading pane. A pen or mouse draws; in stylus mode a finger is treated as a palm.

Leave feedback from the Mac: just feedback "what you saw", then drag a region of the screen.
