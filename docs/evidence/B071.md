## Decisions about the text

Each is a line of code a reviewer can change; a change is a new `content_revision`.

### BSB (S52, `eng`, `ltr`, Protestant order)

1. **The verse bytes come from the publisher's `bsb.txt`, the structure from the USFM.** The brief
   and backlog assumed USFM text. The USFM is generated (`Autogenerated BSB by bsb2usfm`) and its
   running text carries residue `bsb.txt` does not have, in 1,375 verses: spaces around em dashes (about 1,245
   verses), column dots before numbers (11), stray space before punctuation (56), `[’’]` and bracketed
   words (61), and two letter-level differences below. Using `bsb.txt` makes the text the
   publisher's verbatim file, which is also what the naming flag asks of anything called Berean.
2. **The two forms must agree letter for letter or the build stops**, with two recorded exceptions,
   both read against the source: Gen 35:18 (the USFM has a stray `vvv`) and Hab 3:19 (the USFM has
   the closing musical note as a `\mr` heading; `bsb.txt` prints it as the verse's end).
3. **Footnotes** (4,854), **headings** (`\s1 \s2 \ms \mr \qa`, the `\d Psalms 1–41` divisions),
   **cross references** (`\r`, 1,325) and front matter are not text; `bsb.txt` omits them too. The
   format has no place for them, so a reader does not see them yet.
4. **Poetry and paragraphs** set `poetry` and `paragraph_start` on the verse that starts under
   them (`\q1 \q2 \qr \li1 \li2`, `\p \pmo \pc`, a `\b` stanza break, a heading). **Inside a verse
   a line break is one space**, as `bsb.txt` prints it: 25,937 poetry and list lines and 11,624
   paragraph breaks inside verses are not visible in the reading text, because the format is
   verse-level. See "Open" below: this is the largest visible gap.
5. **Psalm titles.** The BSB numbers a title inside verse 1 (`\d \v 1 title`). Of its 116 titled
   Psalms, the canon table numbers verse 0 for 63: there the title becomes `Ps.N.0`
   (`superscription`, no label) and the rest stays `Ps.N.1`; no word moves or changes. In the other
   53 `eng` has no verse 0 and `org` verse 1 holds title and first line alike (Ps 11), so inventing
   one would break the mapping (`Ps.11.0` is `unmapped`). There the title stays at the start of
   verse 1, exactly as `bsb.txt` prints it, not marked as a title. specs/23 §1 now says this.
6. **Tokens**: whitespace fields holding a letter or digit, attached punctuation included, as the
   fixtures tokenize (`Son,`); a free-standing dash takes no number.

### SBLGNT (S06, `org`, `ltr`, Protestant order)

7. **Apparatus signs `⸀ ⸁ ⸂ ⸃ ⸄ ⸅` are removed from the running text** (5,117 / 46 / 1,765 / 1,765 /
   6 / 6), with the number some carry (`⸀1`, `⸀2`: 1 Cor 12:10, 2 Cor 7:12). Reasons: they
   point into an apparatus this pack does not carry; the repository's SBLGNT fixtures already remove
   them (B006), and the placeholder overlay and alignment fixtures are that text; a host would
   otherwise show marks that lead nowhere. This departs from "byte-exact" for those scalars only and
   is the one deliberate edit to any edition's running text; everything else in it is byte-equal. The apparatus
   (`data/sblgntapp`) is under the same licence and can return with its signs when a surface for it exists.
8. **Editorial brackets `⟦ ⟧` and `[ ]` stay**: they say something about the text itself (Mk 16,
   Jn 7:53–8:11). The source's bracket opens in one verse and closes in another; so does the pack's.
9. **The space that ends every verse line is the file's separator**, not text (the fixture agrees).
10. **Paragraphs** come from the XML's `<p>`; 34 fall inside a verse and are not carried. The XML is
    also a second reading: every verse's letters must equal the text file's or the build stops.
11. **Licence.** CC BY 4.0 §3(a) asks for the creator and copyright notice, a licence reference, a
    disclaimer notice, a link where practicable, and an indication of changes. The manifest's
    `attribution` carries the rights record's sentence plus the licence URI, the source and commit,
    the signs removed, and the as-is notice. A host must show it.

### WLC / OSHB (S07, `org`, `rtl`, `tanakh_bhs`)

12. **`/` inside `<w>`** is OSHB markup and is removed; every other scalar stays in source order
    (23,007 verses are not NFC, as the source; nothing is normalized).
13. **Maqqef and sof pasuq attach**; a maqqef binds what follows even past a note (2 Sam 16:23,
    Jer 50:29). **Paseq** and **reversed nun** (9) are text and stay, spaced as the source spaces them.
14. **Petuchah and setumah** (`x-pe` 1,181, `x-samekh` 1,981) are layout, not words: they leave the
    text and start a paragraph at the next verse. 90 fall inside a verse and are counted, not carried.
15. **Small, large and suspended letters** stay in their word; the size is not carried (11).
16. **Ketiv and qere: the written form stays, as the source's main stream has it, unpointed (1,268
    words); the qere (1,260 notes) is not in the reading text.** The rule is one sentence: nothing in a
    `<note>` is reading text. Consequences, stated plainly: a reader sees unpointed ketiv with
    no sign of the qere; 9 words read but not written (Ruth 3:5, 3:17) are invisible; and a search
    for a qere's consonants does not find its verse. See "Open".
17. **Every other note** (`KJV:…`, textual, accent, 3,239 in all) is dropped.
18. **Titles** are Hebrew names from a table in the importer (the source's are English); Psalm titles
    are `superscription` by the canon table's mapping (Ps 3:1, Ps 51:1–2) and keep their `org` number.
    **Poetry** follows the placeholder rule (Job 3:1–42:6, Psalms, Proverbs, Song, Lamentations): the
    source does not mark it.
19. **Tokens**: one per `<w>` of the main stream, no morpheme suffix, as the fixtures have them;
    maqqef, paseq and sof pasuq take no number.

### Search

20. Folding happens only in the search key (`ol-norm-1`); stored bodies equal the chunks' text.
21. What is typed is quoted word by word: no input is a syntax error (tested). `AND`, `OR` and
    `NOT` are words here. The last word is a prefix until a space follows. Phrase hits come first, then
    all the words; BM25, then edition and verse order.
22. **Web: the engine is in the one module** (`--features retrieval`, now what `scripts/wasm-bindings.sh`
    builds, as the Apple and Kotlin scripts do). A second lazily loaded module would avoid the size
    until first search, but the palette asks synchronously inside the core, so it would need the
    Text section reworked as asynchronous across hosts, against the lockstep rule. Keeps ADR 0002's
    wasm32 path. Packs arrive as bytes (`sqlite3_deserialize`) and are held in memory; R7 stays open.

## Rights

The gate passed for all three (status `approved`, hashes equal) and its refusals are tested on made-up
sources: another status, a withheld permission, a changed file, a changed manifest, a wrong file count.
Coverage counts agree with the records: S52 31,086 `\v`; S06 7,939 verses and 137,741 tokens; S07 23,213
verses, and 305,507 tokens plus 1,278 words inside notes = the record's 306,785.

Two things in `rights/S52.json` are for the steward; neither blocks and neither was edited:

- Its `notes` and `counting_rule` say the 16 lines between `\v` markers and `bsb.txt` lines are bridged verses.
  **They are not**: the USFM has no bridge at all; the 16 are empty lines, the verses in the table below.
- Its `attribution` omits two names `bsb.txt`'s own header credits (unfoldingWord, Bible Aquifer). The
  manifest carries the record's sentence as instructed.

The naming flag (Berean name only on unaltered text) is met: every BSB verse line of the pack, with a
split title rejoined, is byte-equal to the publisher's `bsb.txt`.

## Failures and things found on the way

- **The first build stopped on 1,375 BSB differences**: that is how decision 1 was found.
- **`--features retrieval` no longer linked for wasm on main.** Plain `cargo build --release --target
  wasm32-unknown-unknown -p openlogos-ffi --features retrieval` at c0aecb3 fails with undefined `sqlite3_*`:
  `libwsqlite3.a` was a 96-byte empty archive, because the `ar` the shell finds cannot index wasm objects. B064's
  build predates whatever changed. Fixed with `AR_wasm32_unknown_unknown`; no lane caught it because none built the feature.
  Now `just wasm-build` and `just lint-wasm` do.
- **`retrieval_smoke` returned `Result<_, String>`, which UniFFI's generator refuses**, so no binding had ever been generated with the feature on; it now has `RetrievalSmokeError`.
- A refused pack's bytes poisoned the connection (`file is not a database` on every later statement);
  a test caught it, and bytes without the SQLite header are now refused before attaching.

## What the host phase must do

- Install: `open_pack` per `packs/<id>` natively; `addPackManifest`, fetch and `supplyChunk` in the browser.
  No format change needed. `use_edition_search` / `useEditionSearch` for text; fetch `search/*.sqlite` once, in a worker.
- **Show `Edition.attribution`** somewhere reachable: required for the SBLGNT and OSHB.
- Measure text results end to end in each host, with a repaint, against 50 ms, and the Mac app's first (no Swift test calls `use_edition_search` yet).
- Register new revisions: `content_revision` differs from the fixtures' (`bsb-2024`, `sblgnt-2010`, `oshb-wlc-4.20`), so an anchor on an overlay verse reanchors.
- A follower's `segment_id` can name a verse its edition does not print (the shell maps by canon table): scroll to the last verse of the chapter numbered at or below it. Do not assume every `first..last` number exists.
- The WLC is `tanakh_bhs`, so Malachi 3 → Psalms and it ends at 2 Chronicles; the placeholder WLC stays Protestant.

## Open, for the owner or a later task

- **Poetry reads as one run per verse.** The line structure exists in the USFM (decision 4). An additive
  `line_starts` on a verse (scalars, format 1.2) would carry it without touching a byte, once a host can draw it.
  The same mechanism would mark a run-in Psalm title (decision 5).
- **Qere** is neither shown nor searched (decision 16). A notes layer or a search-only table would fix each.
- **Sigla** (decision 7): reversible if the owner wants the signs shown.
- **Hebrew search is whole words**: `ראשית` does not find `בראשית`; the token engine's lemma search is the answer.
- A Hebrew or Greek edition's `chapter_label` is still English (`Psalm 3`, `Chapter 3`) under a heading in its own script; core rule from B078, unchanged.
